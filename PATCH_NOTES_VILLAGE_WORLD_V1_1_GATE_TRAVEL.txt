THE VILLAGE — VILLAGE WORLD V1.1 GATE TRAVEL REPAIR

Village-only changes:
- Replaced the misaligned painted seam connectors with paired district gate portals.
- The Hunter can now physically walk into Old Town's south gate, west bridge, or east gatehouse.
- Each entrance transfers the Hunter to the matching district entrance with a short fade.
- Return gates are active in South, West, and East districts.
- Camera follows the Hunter into the destination district.
- No battle, campaign, card, hunter, relic, economy, or other game mechanics were changed.
