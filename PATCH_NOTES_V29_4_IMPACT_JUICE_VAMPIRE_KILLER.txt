THE VILLAGE V29.4 — IMPACT JUICE + VAMPIRE KILLER

Render-only. No combat values, balance, save schema, or content IDs changed.

STAGE 2 — GAME FEEL
- Directional knockback. e.hitKick was previously applied as a fixed leftward
  nudge regardless of where the shot came from. Enemies are now pushed along the
  actual tower->enemy vector. Visual translation only; pathing is untouched.
- Squash and stretch. A 0.15s squash on impact, scaled around the sprite anchor.
- Directional impact sparks fired in a cone along the hit vector, with per-
  particle size, speed and lifetime variance, and a size falloff as they die.
- Trauma-based screen shake. Shake is now driven by a trauma value that decays
  and is applied as trauma-squared, which reads far better than linear jitter,
  plus a directional impulse so a heavy hit shoves the camera the way the blow
  landed. The pre-existing G.shake calls still work and stack on top.
- Damage numbers with weight. Chip damage is small and muted; solid hits are
  full size; crits are large, outlined, longer-lived, and arc outward with
  horizontal drift and gravity. All numbers now pop in on a cubic ease.
- Death bursts, scaled by rank. Bosses throw roughly three times the debris of
  a common enemy and shake the screen accordingly.

BUG FIXED WHILE DOING THIS
- hit() is called every frame for burn damage, so the old code pushed a damage
  number 60 times a second for every burning enemy. Damage over time now
  accumulates and surfaces one number per 6 damage. This also stops the new
  spark system from spraying particles on every burn tick.

THE SURPRISE — VAMPIRE KILLER MODE
- A homage to Castlevania, which this game was already wearing on its sleeve.
- Enter the Konami code on a keyboard: up up down down left right left right B A.
- On touch, tap the menu crest seven times quickly.
- Unlocking it lights a gothic rose window over the battlefield in slowly
  rotating stained glass, turns every impact spark holy gold, and lets the night
  speak up every fourth wave.
- It is a toggle. Enter it again to seal the whip away.
- The unlock persists under its own localStorage key, 'village.vk.mode'. It does
  NOT touch your save object, so save compatibility is unaffected either way.

TUNING
- Every value lives in the JUICE constant at the top of the V29.4 block: spark
  lifetime and speed, cone width, knockback cap and decay, squash time and
  amount, trauma decay, shake amplitude, and the three damage-number sizes.

VERIFICATION PERFORMED
- node --check passes.
- 22 unit tests on the extracted juice module: damage-over-time throttling,
  spark counts scaling with damage, crit trauma exceeding chip trauma,
  knockback direction resolving correctly on both sides of a tower, kick
  magnitude clamping, damage-number sizing and crit flagging, trauma clamping
  and decay to rest, shake finiteness at rest and at maximum trauma, the
  Vampire Killer toggle and its persistence, homage lines staying silent when
  the mode is off, and death bursts scaling with rank. All pass.
- The 20 V29.3 animation tests were re-run against the patched file: no
  regressions.
- The damage-number pop curve was swept across its full range for all three
  sizes: no non-finite or out-of-range values, and legacy floaters with no size
  or pop field still render at their original scale.

NOT PERFORMED
- No in-browser run. Shake amplitude and knockback distance are the two values
  most likely to want tuning by eye on a real device.
