THE VILLAGE V29.6 — CARDS SCREEN REBUILD

Presentation only. No card, tower, rarity, merge, JP or save logic was touched.
No HTML or JavaScript was changed for this rebuild.

=== WHAT WAS WRONG ===
The Cards screen had been restyled by appending override blocks rather than
editing existing rules, over many iterations:

  1,054 of 2,756 rules in styles.css were #deckScreen-scoped   (38% of them)
  154,690 bytes of 309,734                                     (50% of the file)
  3,593 of 3,860 declarations carried !important                (93%)
  #deckScreen #collectionCards.cards.portrait-grid appeared 38 times
  #deckScreen #equippedDeck.equipped-deck                       29 times
  #deckScreen #equippedDeck .deck-slot                          28 times

Once a rule is declared 38 times with !important, nothing wins predictably.
That is the root cause of every symptom in the screenshots.

=== ROOT CAUSE OF THE COLOUR INCONSISTENCY ===
.rarity-common / good / rare / epic / legendary defined only --rarity.
.rarity-epicplus / legendaryplus / mythic defined --rarity AND an inset
box-shadow marked !important. That inset shadow painted over the card fill, so
the three highest tiers rendered dark with a coloured ring while every lower
tier rendered as a filled colour card. Epic+ and Legendary+ looked like a
different game to Epic and Rare.

Fixed: every tier now derives its fill, border, art panel, badge and footer
colour from the same var(--rarity) recipe. Tier identity is now ADDITIVE on
top of that fill, never a replacement:
  - all eight tiers: rarity-tinted gradient body, border and art panel
  - epic+, legendary, legendary+, mythic: an extra inner rarity ring
  - legendary, legendary+, mythic: a slow animated foil sweep
  - mythic sweeps faster and brighter
The equipped slots and the collection cards now share one gradient recipe, so
a card looks identical wherever it appears.

=== BUGS FIXED FROM THE SCREENSHOTS ===
- Equipped top row rendered as bare icons. The equipped slots are built by
  miniCardHTML() with a completely different class set (deck-card-art,
  deck-card-name, deck-card-stats, deck-card-view) from the collection cards
  built by cardHTML(). Those five classes had lost all styling in the override
  spiral, and the slots were being forced to a fixed aspect-ratio with
  overflow:hidden, so the name and stats were clipped away. All five are now
  styled, rows are equal height, and nothing is clipped.
- The bottom navigation covered the last row of cards. The panel now reserves
  96px plus the iOS safe-area inset.
- Rarity name and copy count in the card footer were dark-on-dark and
  unreadable. The rarity name now renders in its own rarity colour with a soft
  glow; the copy count sits at readable contrast.
- The Active Loadout left a large empty column beside the hero panel. The
  loadout is now a two-column grid: hero panel fixed, cards fill the rest.
- The deck profile box mangled its text into a narrow column. It is now a
  wrapping horizontal strip.

=== THE REBUILD ===
All 1,054 #deckScreen rules were removed and replaced with one authoritative
block of 179 rules at the end of styles.css.

                         before      after
  #deckScreen rules       1,054        179
  declarations            3,860        815
  !important                 93%         1%   (4 declarations, only to defeat
                                               the three global rarity shadows)
  styles.css            309,734    191,823    (38% smaller)

Nothing outside the Cards screen was touched: every non-#deckScreen selector in
the file is byte-identical, verified by re-parsing.

=== JUICE ===
- Cards fade and rise in on a staggered cubic ease.
- Hover lifts the card 6px with a rarity-coloured glow; the sigil scales and
  tilts. Press gives a physical scale-down.
- Legendary and above carry a continuous foil sweep.
- Available job nodes pulse gold.
- Tabs, buttons and slots all animate on hover and press.
- Everything animated is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

=== SAFARI SAFETY ===
The design uses color-mix(), which needs Safari 16.2+. Every critical
declaration (card background, border, art panel, footer colour) is preceded by
a plain-colour fallback, so on an older engine the cards render in neutral
slate rather than losing their background entirely.

=== VERIFICATION PERFORMED ===
- 28 automated CSS assertions, all passing: rarity consistency across all eight
  tiers, both card bodies sharing one recipe, no tier replacing the fill,
  fallbacks present ahead of every color-mix, each screenshot bug closed,
  each juice effect present, reduced-motion honoured, braces balanced, and no
  duplicate selector storms remaining.
- Coverage check: all 147 classes in the Cards screen markup were cross-checked
  against the new block. Every element that had styling before still has it.
- Re-parse confirms 0 non-deck selectors lost.

=== NOT PERFORMED ===
- No in-browser run. Layout numbers (card minimum width, grid gaps, the 96px
  nav reserve) are the values most likely to want adjusting by eye. They are
  all at the top of the block in the #deckScreen custom properties.
