THE VILLAGE V29.8 — DISTRICT SEAM, BUILD ROSTER, TACTICAL ESPIONAGE

=== 1. THE DISTRICT MISALIGNMENT ===
Diagnosed in a real browser, not guessed at.

The DOM grid was never wrong. Measured on screen, the four district frames tile
perfectly: west.right = oldTown.left = 476.9, oldTown.right = east.left = 893.8,
oldTown.bottom = south.top = 456.9. The JS coordinate system (WORLD 5322x2661,
districts at 1774x887) agrees with the CSS exactly.

The seam came from the ARTWORK. Content bounding boxes:
  west_district.png    1774x887   margins L0  R0  T0  B0   full bleed
  east_district.png    1774x887   margins L0  R0  T0  B0   full bleed
  south_district.png   1774x887   margins L0  R0  T0  B0   full bleed
  old_town_ground.png  1774x887   margins L51 R16 T13 B29  <-- inset
Old Town alone had a dead border baked into the image, so it never met its
neighbours no matter what the layout did.

FIXED BY EDGE-EXTENSION, NOT RESCALING. The outermost real row and column of
pixels are stretched outward to fill the dead border. Every art feature stays
at exactly the coordinate it was at before, which means plot positions, gate
portals, collision boxes and Kael's pathing are all untouched. Rescaling the
image would have shifted features by up to 60px and broken the plot alignment
that TOWN_V3_1 and V3_2 were spent tuning.
  old_town_ground.png  -> margins L0 R0 T0 B0
  village_ground.png   -> margins L0 R0 T0 B0  (same source art, same defect)
Verified in-browser by sampling the image edges: 0 dark pixels on all four sides.

=== 2. THE BUILD MENU ===
It had exactly two structures. BUILDINGS contained house and farm and nothing
else, and the tray was hardcoded HTML listing those same two.

Now 26 structures across 7 categories, and the tray renders FROM the data, so
the menu and the game can never disagree again:
  Residential  House* · Manor* · Almshouse
  Agriculture  Farm Plot* · Orchard · Herb Garden · Stone Well
  Industry     Sawmill · Quarry · Blacksmith · Tannery
  Military     Watchtower · Barracks · Armory · Palisade
  Sacred       Chapel · Roadside Shrine · Reliquary · Graveyard
  Arcane       Alchemist · Observatory · Rune Stone
  Civic        Market · Tavern · Storehouse · Bathhouse
  (* uses real art; the other 23 render a placeholder tile)

Placeholders are deliberate and legible: a hatched tile, the structure's icon,
and a small ART PENDING label. They are fully functional right now — you can
select and place a Quarry today. To promote one to real art, add a single
`image:` field to its entry in BUILDINGS. No other change needed.

Every card shows name, output and cost. Categories are collapsible accordions.

=== 3. KONAMI — TACTICAL ESPIONAGE VILLAGE ===
Up Up Down Down Left Right Left Right B A in the village. On touch, seven quick
taps on the village crest.
  - the alert "!" pops over Kael, scaled on a spring curve
  - Kael deploys a cardboard box, which bobs
  - the world desaturates and the economy strip goes codec green
  - the Colonel checks in occasionally with a line
Persists under its own localStorage key, 'village.tactical.mode'. It does not
touch the save object. Enter the code again to stand down.
