THE VILLAGE V32.1 — A NEW BEGINNING

Everything below was verified in a real headless browser, on a wiped save, at
PC 1600x900, iPad 1366x1024 @2x and Phone 430x932 @3x.

=== 1. A GENUINELY FRESH START ===
A brand new hunter now owns EIGHT cards. Nothing else.
  Whip Tower · Holy Water Infusion · Dagger Tower        (three defenses)
  Holy Water Barrage · Cross Storm · Frost Ward          (three skills)
  Road Spikes · Oil Trap                                 (the two ground slots)

Three separate systems were handing out cards on the very first launch:
  a) HERO_GROUND_DEFENSES ran "if(item.copies<1)item.copies=1" over ALL NINE
     traps unconditionally. Seven of them are now withheld.
  b) garlic was force-granted two copies at load.
  c) scripture was pushed into save.unlocked unconditionally, and a separate
     loop grants one copy to everything in save.unlocked — so it arrived on the
     next launch even after its own grant was gated.
All three now respect campaign progression.

The withheld cards were not deleted, they were placed on the road:
  Chapter I   Cemetery   axe · bone · garlic · Bear Trap
  Chapter II  Forest     rosary · guardian · Frost Rune
  Chapter III Village    cross · axeRain · Poison Fog
  Chapter IV  Cathedral  clock · soulPact · scripture
  Chapter V   Marsh      familiar · sharpen · Holy Banner
  Chapter VI  Walls      silver · fortify · Watch Bell
  Chapter VII Temple     summon · moonBlessing · Shadow Mine
  Chapter VIII Castle    grandCross · Soul Lantern
Verified: clearing Chapter I grants exactly axe, bone, garlic and Bear Trap,
and Chapter VI's silver plus Chapter IV's scripture remain locked away.

=== 2. THE ENEMY CURVE NOW ACTUALLY PROGRESSES ===
This was the biggest hole. Enemy HP was:
     base * (1 + wave*0.20) * 3.35
There was NO chapter term at all. A Chapter 20 wave 1 Bone Soldier had exactly
the same health as a Chapter 1 wave 1 Bone Soldier. The only thing that grew
across twenty roads was the boss.

Now:
     base * waveCurve * chapterCurve * 3.05
     waveCurve    = 1 + (wave-1)^1.08 * 0.19
     chapterCurve = 1.09^(chapter-1)
Enemies also hit slightly harder each chapter (+4% per road).

  Bone Soldier effective HP
              w1     w5    w10    w20    w30
    ch  1     92    169    278    510    752
    ch  5    129    239    392    719   1061
    ch 10    199    367    604   1107   1632
    ch 20    470    870   1430   2620   3864

The opening battle is 24% gentler than before, which matters when the player
now starts with three defenses instead of ten. The campaign span went from
5.8x to 42x. Every constant lives in one DIFFICULTY table above spawnEnemy.

=== 3. OPENING STORY ===
Four beats on first launch: The Last Safe Haven, The Hunter, The Road Brings
the Night, Build by Day Hunt by Night. Skippable, paged, and it never replays
once seen (save.introSeen).

=== 4. A LIVING ROADMAP ===
The prologue hands off to a six-step checklist that reads real save state, so
nothing is ticked until it is genuinely earned:
  1. Assemble your Battle Deck            (deck has six cards)
  2. Clear Chapter I                      (campaign.completed includes cemetery)
  3. Claim the Heart of the Golem         (villageProgression.artifacts)
  4. Shadow awakens to Level II           (shadowAwakeningComplete / shadowLevel)
  5. Raise your first structure           (a placed village plot)
  6. Complete your first research project (villageProgression.researched)
It refreshes every four seconds and on tab focus. A corner button reopens it
with a live count, and it hides itself once all six are done.
