THE VILLAGE — V31.4 FF4 CLOSE CAMERA

CAMERA
- Increased the fixed Village camera scale for a much closer classic-JRPG presentation.
- The full map is no longer visible at once on tablet/desktop screens.
- Added smooth frame-rate-independent camera following.
- Added a small dead zone to prevent constant camera jitter.
- Added directional look-ahead while Shadow walks.
- Added soft camera settling when Shadow stops.
- Camera recenters cleanly after viewport resizing or orientation changes.

INPUT
- Village pinch-to-zoom remains disabled.
- Village mouse-wheel zoom remains disabled.
- Manual Village camera dragging/panning remains disabled.
- Camera distance is fixed by the game rather than controlled by the player.

PRESERVED
- Full-map collision mask.
- Shadow movement and animation.
- Construction plots and building systems.
- Living Village citizens and atmosphere.
- Day/night, weather, interactions, and exploration rewards.
- Cards, battles, progression, economy, and existing save format.
