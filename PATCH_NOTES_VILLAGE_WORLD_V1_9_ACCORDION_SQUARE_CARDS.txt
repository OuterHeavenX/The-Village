THE VILLAGE — VILLAGE WORLD V1.9

Village-only UI update:
- Rebuilt Build Mode as a compact accordion menu by category.
- Added collapsible Residential, Production, Arcane, Commerce, Military, and Civic sections.
- Existing structures now display as square picture cards rather than wide rows.
- Phone layout uses two columns; tablet layout uses up to four.
- Selecting a structure still closes the menu and activates plot placement.
- No campaign, battle, cards, hunters, relics, economy, or other non-Village mechanics changed.
