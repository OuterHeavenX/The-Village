THE VILLAGE V29.3 — ENEMY ANIMATION STATES

Visual only. No combat values, balance, save schema, or content IDs changed.

THE PROBLEM
- The project ships 240 combat sprite sheets. Code referenced 11 of them (4.6%).
- Every non-boss enemy drew only its Walk or Run sheet. The Attack, Hurt, Death
  and Idle sheets — 55 Attack, 33 Hurt, 33 Death, 33 Idle — never drew a frame.
- Enemies never swung, never flinched when shot, and never died on screen; they
  slid in walk-cycle until they were removed from the array.

WHAT WAS ADDED
- ENEMY_FAMILY: maps all 15 enemy types to their sprite family, handling the two
  different filename conventions (orc sheets are lowercase, all others are
  capitalised).
- enemyAsset(type, action) resolves any type to any of its 6 action sheets.
- Per-enemy animation clock (e.animT) and hurt timer (e.hurtT).
- Action state machine: Attack > Hurt > Idle (frozen) > Walk.
- Enemies now play their real Attack sheet when striking the cathedral gate.
- Enemies now flinch on the Hurt sheet for 0.26s when damaged.
- Frozen enemies now hold on the Idle sheet instead of moonwalking.
- Death animations: on death a purely visual corpse entity plays the Death sheet
  through, then fades over 0.9s.

WHY DEATHS ARE A SEPARATE ENTITY
- Gameplay removal of the enemy is completely unchanged. The corpse is a
  render-only object in G.corpses. It cannot be hit, cannot block, cannot be
  targeted, and carries no combat state. This guarantees the death animation
  cannot alter mechanics or timing.
- Corpses are capped at 40 and self-expire, so long waves cannot leak memory.

BOSSES
- Golems already switched sheets and keep doing so. Golem sheets ship only
  Walk/Idle/Attack/Death, so a golem Hurt request falls back to Walk rather than
  requesting a file that does not exist.

VERIFICATION PERFORMED
- node --check passes.
- All 90 generated enemy paths (15 types x 6 actions) and all 12 golem paths
  were checked against the shipped files: 0 missing.
- 20 unit tests on the extracted animation module: state machine priority,
  frame-bounds clamping across the full hurt curve, sheet-name casing per
  family, unknown-type fallback, golem Hurt->Walk remap, corpse lifecycle,
  corpse cap, and end-of-animation frame clamp plus alpha fade. All pass.

NOT PERFORMED
- No in-browser run. Please check frame pacing on device; ENEMY_ANIM at the top
  of the animation block holds every timing value (hurt duration, attack/walk/
  idle/death fps, fade length, corpse cap) if anything needs tuning by eye.

STILL UNUSED
- The zombie (Zombie1-3) and floating-eye (Beholder1-3) families are fully
  animated and shipped but no enemy type maps to them: 36 more sheets available
  whenever you want new enemies.
