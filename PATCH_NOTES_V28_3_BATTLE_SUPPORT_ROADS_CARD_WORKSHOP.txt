THE VILLAGE V28.3 — BATTLE SUPPORT, RANDOM ROADS & CARD WORKSHOP

BATTLE UPGRADES
- Tower upgrades are now card-wide for the current hunt.
- Upgrading Axe to Level 2 upgrades every deployed Axe Tower.
- Future Axe Towers placed during that hunt also inherit Level 2.
- The same rule applies to every exact attack-tower card type.
- Axe remains the only tower with a purchasable attack-radius upgrade.

SUPPORT TOWERS
- Fixed support cards not being registered on their target tower.
- Holy Water Infusion now provides a stronger damage/attack-speed boost and activates burn effects.
- Chrono Sigil now provides stronger attack-speed, range, and slowing support.
- Guardian Ward now provides stronger combat support and restores Cathedral health between waves.
- Support placement now confirms that the link is active.

ROAD GENERATION
- Replaced predictable edge-to-edge serpentine plans with randomized connected waypoints.
- Routes now vary in bend direction, run length, reversal timing, overall silhouette, and split-route activation wave.
- Every generated road remains continuously connected.

CARDS SCREEN
- Rebuilt the presentation as the Gothic Card Workshop.
- Cleaner command header and build counter.
- Stronger separation between the hunter loadout, six-card deck, filters, tools, and collection.
- Improved gothic hierarchy, spacing, card selection feedback, and responsive mobile/iPad layouts.
- Updated build identification to V28.3.

COMPATIBILITY
- Existing save structure remains intact.
- Battle card-wide upgrades remain run-only and reset after the hunt.
