# V35.2 campaign battlefield and balance audit

Generated by `npm run audit:campaign` with 1,200 deterministic reasonable-play runs per stage plus 200 high-power stability runs. These are comparative engineering models, not a substitute for human playtesting.

## Runtime corrections

- The painted matte is the physical battlefield. Normal play no longer renders a global grid, path rectangles, cathedral mesh, Keep façade, collision zones, or persistent range geometry.
- The cathedral destination remains a logical zone at the head of the path. Only aligned cracks, smoke, and fire damage overlays may render.
- Roads are continuous multiply-blended wear decals. Logical path nodes remain invisible.
- Placement foundations are 2.5% opacity while inactive and become readable only during placement.
- Structure limits are maxima rather than required role slots.

## Scaling correction

Normal enemy chapter compounding changed from 1.09 to 1.055. Bosses now use a separate 1.25 baseline because their authored HP already increases by stage; they no longer inherit the 3.05 normal-enemy density multiplier. Existing save power and owned cards are unchanged.

Stage 10 now owns the one-time Dracula's Tooth awakening trigger. Existing saves with the Tooth or Shadow Level II remain untouched.
