THE VILLAGE V29.5 — THE DEEP ROSTER, VISIBLE SYNERGY, BATTLEFIELD JUICE

=== 1. CAMPAIGN EXTENDED TO TWENTY CHAPTERS ===
- Chapters 1-10 are untouched. The original roster still owns the early road.
- Chapters 11-20 draw from a deep roster that mixes the old enemies with six
  new ones. The gate is a single check on chapter number, so nothing about the
  first ten chapters changed.
- Eight new chapters (12-19) with their own maps, bosses, relics and lore.
- The Eclipse Throne moved from chapter 12 to chapter 20 and its boss was
  rescaled from 4,450 HP to 11,000 to sit at the end of a twenty-chapter road.
- Eight new maps and eight new relics. No map or relic is used twice.

THE SIX NEW ENEMIES (chapter 11 onward)
  Ravenous Ghoul    44hp  slow, cheap, arrives in numbers
  Plague Revenant   82hp  28% armour
  Gravelord        145hp  46% armour, very slow, a wall
  Pale Watcher      36hp  FLYING, fastest thing on the field
  Void Gazer        64hp  FLYING, holy damage only
  Dread Eye        104hp  FLYING, tanky
- The three eyes fly, which means your existing air-targeting rules apply to
  them. The Void Gazer takes holy damage only, so chapters 15+ punish a deck
  with no holy answer.

BUG FOUND AND FIXED
- Four bosses had no sprite family entry: bellkeeper (ch9), ashabbot (ch10),
  underking (ch11) and eclipselord — your FINAL BOSS. All four were silently
  falling back to the orc sprite. They now have proper vampire and orc families.

=== 2. SYNERGY IS NOW VISIBLE ===
- Active synergies draw as a glowing thread between the two towers, with a mote
  travelling FROM the partner INTO the tower being buffed, so the direction of
  the bonus is readable at a glance.
- Every tower currently receiving a bonus wears a soft pulsing ring.
- Each synergy has its own colour and name: Holy Infusion, Frost Channel,
  Guardian Watch, Blessed Blades, Sanctified Time, Bone Ignition, Rosary Cadence.
- Selecting a tower now spells out exactly what it is getting and from whom,
  e.g. "Holy Infusion + Bone Ignition / +70% damage · +22% fire rate / from
  Holy Water Infusion, Bone Pillar" instead of the old "Synergy active".
- With no synergy it now tells you how to make one rather than just saying no.
- A full synergy legend was added to the in-game tutorial listing all seven
  combinations and their exact numbers.

HOW THIS WAS KEPT HONEST
- The combat function synergyFor() was NOT modified. The display reads a
  separate mirror function. A test compares the two across all 16,807 possible
  tower-and-neighbour configurations: they agree exactly. The display can
  never show a bonus the game is not actually applying.

=== 3. BATTLEFIELD JUICE ===
- Damage numbers no longer stack. Your screenshot showed three numbers piled on
  the same pixel; they now fan out horizontally and stagger vertically.
- Towers recoil away from their target when they fire, with a muzzle flash and
  a spark puff. Synergised towers throw a brighter, larger flash.
- The gate bleeds. Taking damage at the cathedral washes the screen edges red
  and shoves the camera; bosses hit noticeably harder than common enemies.

=== NOT CHANGED ===
- Chapters 1-10 in every respect. Existing tower stats, card definitions, save
  schema, and all synergy multipliers.
