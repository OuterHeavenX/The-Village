# V33.0.0 — The Living Village

Implemented directly on the V32.7.1 citizen-fix branch contained in the uploaded ZIP.

## New systems
- Accelerated day/night clock with dawn, day, dusk, and night lighting.
- Lamps activate automatically at night.
- Citizens now have jobs and time-based destinations, including farmers, guards, merchants, a priest, workers, children, and specialists.
- Citizens return home at night and gather near the town center during evening hours.
- A lightweight familiar companion follows Shadow throughout the Three.js Village.
- Newly placed buildings now rise through a visible construction sequence rather than appearing instantly.
- Building interaction opens compact FF-style interior scenes.
- Cathedral receives a dedicated blessing interaction and story-facing description.
- Keep, houses, farms, Library, Blacksmith, and Tavern receive contextual interior descriptions.
- Existing rain, fireflies, smoke, animated crops, citizens, saves, resources, plots, battle systems, and MGS easter eggs remain intact.
