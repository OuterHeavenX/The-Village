THE VILLAGE V32.4 — FAMILIARS & KEEP EVOLUTION

SHADOW FAMILIARS
- Added five personal familiars that orbit and follow Shadow in battle:
  Night Bat, Giant Sword, Demon, Ghost, and Faerie.
- Added a Familiar selection area to the Heroes screen.
- Only one familiar is equipped at a time.
- Every familiar has its own persistent level and XP bar.
- Familiar XP is earned from enemy defeats while that familiar is equipped.
- Familiar levels are preserved in the existing save file.
- Each familiar has a distinct autonomous role:
  Bat: rapid strikes.
  Giant Sword: heavy slashes.
  Demon: damaging attacks that burn targets.
  Ghost: attacks that slow targets.
  Faerie: periodically heals the Keep.

BATTLE KEEP EVOLUTION
- The battle Keep now visually grows through five presentation levels.
- Existing Reinforced Keep progression controls the battle Keep level.
- Higher levels add larger walls, battlements, towers, banners, and archers.
- Level 1 has no archers; Levels 2–5 add one additional archer per level.
- Archers automatically target enemies approaching the Keep.
- Higher Keep levels improve archer damage, range, and volley speed.

COMPATIBILITY
- Existing campaign, cards, buildings, Shadow progress, and saves are preserved.
- No card layout or Dagger Tower behavior was changed.
