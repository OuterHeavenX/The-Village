RELICS OF THE KINGDOM — V16.0 ESSENCE DRAFT

- Removed the permanent card toolbar from battle.
- Added a 40-point Essence Vial to the battle HUD.
- The run begins with a full vial, granting one opening card draft.
- Defeated enemies release Essence that flies into the vial.
- At 40 Essence the battle pauses and three random cards are shown.
- Choosing a card resets the vial, allows one free placement/cast, then resumes battle.
- Wave completion no longer grants a card.
- Existing combat, cathedral, camera, traps, supports, and Scripture Tower remain intact.
