THE VILLAGE V32.2 — CLEAN SLATE

=== WHY YOUR PROGRESS SURVIVED ===
V32.1's gates only decide what a NEW save is handed. Your save already existed,
so it kept everything it had already earned. That was intended, but it is not
what you wanted, so this build ships one guaranteed clean slate.

FRESH_START_TOKEN. The first time V32.2 runs it clears every key written by any
previous build, then records its token so it never wipes again. Launch it once
and you are genuinely at night one. Everything you do after that is kept
normally — verified by making progress after the wipe and relaunching.

=== WHY THE RESET BUTTON KILLED THE TAB ===
Two real faults, both fixed.

1. IT ONLY CLEARED TWO KEYS OUT OF SIX.
   The handler removed 'relicsEclipseSave' and 'gateRunnerSave'. The game also
   writes 'relicsEclipseSave_backup_v12', 'theVillageFreshTownV1Plots',
   'rotkVillagePlots' and 'rotkVillagePlotsV224B', plus the easter-egg flags.
   So after a "full reset" your village was still full of buildings that the
   brand-new save had no research for. Confirmed in a browser: all three plot
   stores survived the reset untouched.

2. IT USED A NATIVE confirm() AND THEN RELOADED STRAIGHT INTO IT.
   confirm() blocks the main thread while the render loop, the audio graph and
   the village timers keep queueing work behind it. location.reload() then tore
   the document down mid-frame with all of that still live. On a memory-tight
   iPad with a dozen tabs open, that is a very good way to lose the tab.

Now:
   - an in-page confirmation styled like the rest of the game, no native modal
   - the battle loop is paused and the audio graph silenced BEFORE anything is
     torn down
   - every key is cleared by explicit list AND by prefix sweep, so nothing that
     belongs to this game can survive
   - it navigates with location.replace() to a fresh URL rather than reloading,
     which guarantees a clean document instead of a possible bfcache restore
   - every step is wrapped, so a failure in one cannot strand the reset

=== ONE THING TO KNOW ===
Because this build wipes once on first launch, do not launch it until you are
ready to lose your current progress. If you want to keep your current save,
open src/Battle/game.js and delete the FRESH_START_TOKEN block near the top of
the save loader; the repaired Reset button will still be there when you want it.
