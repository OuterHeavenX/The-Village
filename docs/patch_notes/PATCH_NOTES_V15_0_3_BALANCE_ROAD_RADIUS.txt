RELICS OF THE KINGDOM — V15.0.3
BALANCE, ROAD & RADIUS FIX

- Tower selection radius display reduced by another 60% from V15.0.2.
- Starting roads now leave the gate as one clean path.
- Starting-road generation rejects cells that touch older road cells, preventing doubled or parallel strips.
- Standard enemy health increased substantially.
- Wave health scaling increased.
- Elite enemies are tougher and appear slightly more often in later waves.
- Bosses and summoned enemies received matching durability increases.
- Deck fixes from V15.0.2 remain unchanged.
