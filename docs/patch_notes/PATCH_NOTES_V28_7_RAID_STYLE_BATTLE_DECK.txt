THE VILLAGE — V28.7 RAID-STYLE BATTLE DECK

CARDS UI
- Replaced the V28.6 Cards layout with a new icon-first battle deck structure inspired by the clarity of modern mobile tower-defense deck screens.
- The selected hunter is now a large featured loadout card beside a 3x2 six-card battle deck.
- Battle deck cards now emphasize icon artwork, card level, ATK, and HP with minimal text.
- Collection cards now use larger fully visible icons, full rarity-colored backgrounds, readable levels, ATK, and HP.
- Category tabs remain separate for All Cards, Defense, Support, Battle Skills, Run Upgrades, and Passives.
- Existing loadouts, two passive slots, merge controls, sorting, favorites, card inspection, and saves remain compatible.
- Responsive layouts added for iPad, phones, and narrow portrait screens.

SYSTEMS
- No battle mechanics, campaign progression, support behavior, road generation, boss progression, or save keys were removed.
- Card merging still permanently increases card level and power.
