THE VILLAGE V32.2.2 — BATTLE EXIT + AOE FIX

BATTLE COMPLETION
- Replaced the raw enemy-array-length victory condition with a living-enemy check.
- Victory is now evaluated before pause handling, so an Essence draft cannot deadlock the final wave.
- Victory is also evaluated immediately after dead enemies are removed in the same frame.
- Added a one-time completion guard to prevent duplicate rewards or duplicate result transitions.
- Added a recovery result screen if an unexpected completion exception occurs.
- Pending placement, draft, tower-inspector, and selection states are cleared at victory.
- Delayed Essence pickups can no longer reopen a draft after completion begins.

TOWER AOE / ATTACK RADIUS
- All towers now use 50% of their previous effective attack radius.
- Radius upgrades, synergies, weather, and relic range modifiers scale from the new halved radius.
- Explicit splash radii were also reduced by 50%:
  Holy Water Infusion: 1.00 -> 0.50
  Silver Cannon: 1.10 -> 0.55
  Garlic Tower splash value: 0.72 -> 0.36

SAVE DATA
- Existing saves and campaign progress remain compatible.
