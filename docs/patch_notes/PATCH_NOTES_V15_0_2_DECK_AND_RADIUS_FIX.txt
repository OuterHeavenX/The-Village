RELICS OF THE KINGDOM — V15.0.2

Focused stability patch:
- Reduced the selected-tower range overlay to 40% of its previous visual size (60% smaller).
- Fixed removing cards from equipped deck slots.
- Fixed Equip/Remove buttons in the card collection.
- Fixed Equip/Remove from the card detail screen.
- Removed the render-time auto-refill behavior that caused removed cards to immediately reappear.
- Preserved the six-card maximum and Save Deck persistence.

No unrelated gameplay changes were made.
