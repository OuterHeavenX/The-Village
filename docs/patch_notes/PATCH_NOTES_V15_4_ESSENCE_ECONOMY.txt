RELICS OF THE KINGDOM — V15.4 ESSENCE ECONOMY

IMPLEMENTED
- Continuous Essence economy replaces automatic post-wave tower rewards.
- Hunts begin with 10 / 60 Essence: enough for two 5-Essence Daggers.
- Enemy Essence rewards scale from common enemies through elites, mini-bosses and bosses.
- Defeated enemies launch animated Essence motes toward the top HUD pool.
- All six equipped battle-deck cards remain available during combat and may be purchased whenever Essence is sufficient.
- Two hero bonus ground-defense cards are available outside the six-card deck:
  * Road Spikes: repeated road damage.
  * Grave Oil: road slow with light damage and ignition chance.
- Ground defenses place directly on road cells.
- Support cards now create physical support towers beside attack towers instead of attaching invisibly.
- Support towers boost adjacent attack-tower damage, attack speed, range or holy effects depending on type.
- Inventory cards, equipped-deck cards and battle cards now use a vertical poker-card ratio.
- Equipped deck cards inherit the same rarity color as the owned inventory card, including level/rarity consistency.

BALANCE FOUNDATION
Dagger 5, Whip 7, Garlic 7, basic supports 8–9, heavy defenses 10–15, Living Word 18.
Common enemies yield roughly 1 Essence, armored/vampire enemies 2+, elites more, mini-bosses about 8, and bosses scale far higher.
