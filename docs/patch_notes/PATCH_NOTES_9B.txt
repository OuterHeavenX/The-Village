RELICS OF THE KINGDOM — MILESTONE 9B
Campaign Card Unlock Fix

- Six starter cards are available from the beginning.
- New cards now unlock permanently on the first clear of each campaign chapter.
- Chapter 1 immediately awards Axe Tower and Bone Pillar.
- Each campaign node now shows the cards it awards.
- Locked cards remain visible in the collection with their unlock requirement.
- Victory results clearly announce newly unlocked cards.
- Reward chests now give duplicates only from cards already unlocked.
- Existing saves are migrated using completed chapters, so cleared stages award their intended cards.
