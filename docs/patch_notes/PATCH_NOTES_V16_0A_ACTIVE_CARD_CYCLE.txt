RELICS OF THE KINGDOM — V16.0a ACTIVE CARD CYCLE

- The Essence vial no longer resets when the three-card draft opens.
- At 40 Essence, choose one of three random cards.
- The chosen card becomes the only active card for that draft cycle.
- Every placement/cast spends that card's Essence cost.
- You may place multiple copies of the chosen card while you can afford it.
- Other cards cannot be placed until the vial refills to 40 and a new draft appears.
- The active card remains visible above the battlefield and can be tapped again.
- Example: 40 Essence + Dagger costing 20 = up to two Dagger placements.
