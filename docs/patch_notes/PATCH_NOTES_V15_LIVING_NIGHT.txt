- 20% enemy-health increase plus faster wave spawning and random elite enemies.

- Combat skills selected between waves are armed instead of wasted when no enemies exist; they trigger when the next enemy enters.
- New Garlic Tower added and automatically unlocked with two copies.
- Garlic Tower damages and slows all undead inside its aura.
- Tap any placed tower to show its real attack radius on the battlefield.
- Added combo announcements for rapid kills.
- Added explicit Axe Rain and Grand Cross combat effects.
- Existing living atmosphere systems retained: weather, fog, rain, blood moon, particles, animated tower art, boss cinematics, relic discoveries, elite tower rewards, critical hits, and reactive cathedral.
