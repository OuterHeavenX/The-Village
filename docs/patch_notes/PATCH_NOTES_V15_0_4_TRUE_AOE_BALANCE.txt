RELICS OF THE KINGDOM — V15.0.4 TRUE AOE & BALANCE FIX

- The visible tower radius is now the exact gameplay attack radius.
- Towers only acquire enemies inside the displayed AOE.
- Projectiles no longer damage enemies that leave the tower AOE before impact.
- Splash damage is clipped to the firing tower's displayed AOE.
- All tower AOE starts at approximately 1.35 tiles, with only small level growth.
- Enemy health and wave scaling increased substantially.
- Summoned enemies were strengthened.
- V15.0.2 deck fixes and V15.0.3 road fixes are preserved.
