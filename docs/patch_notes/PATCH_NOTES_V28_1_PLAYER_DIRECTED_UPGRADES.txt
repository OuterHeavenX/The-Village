THE VILLAGE V28.1 — PLAYER-DIRECTED BATTLE UPGRADES

Implemented:
- Removed kill-owner tower XP and automatic tower leveling.
- All enemy deaths now award shared Battle XP, regardless of which tower or Shadow lands the final hit.
- Filling the Battle XP bar awards Upgrade Points controlled by the player.
- Added an UPGRADES counter to the battle HUD.
- Tap/click a placed attack tower to spend points on one of three paths:
  * Power: +24% damage
  * Range: +0.34 tiles
  * Speed: approximately 16% faster attacks
- Upgrade costs scale as tower level rises.
- Towers cap at level 6 during the current battle.
- Normal enemies grant 1 Battle XP, elites 2, minibosses 4, and bosses 6.
- Blood Moon now boosts shared Battle XP instead of tower-owned XP.
- Thornheart Crown now begins a hunt with one Upgrade Point.

AXE TOWER RANGE:
- Range calculation now respects each card's actual range stat.
- The Axe Tower receives a larger +0.50 tile gain whenever the player chooses Range.
- Its high-arc behavior allows it to remain useful when placed behind another tower.

COMPATIBILITY:
- Existing permanent card progression, campaign progress, boss transformations, Shadow Level 2 unlock, tower merging, maps, deck, and save data remain intact.
- Upgrade Points and chosen battle upgrades are run-only and are not written into permanent save data.
