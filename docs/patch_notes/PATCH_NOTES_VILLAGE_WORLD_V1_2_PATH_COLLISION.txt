THE VILLAGE — VILLAGE WORLD V1.2 PATH COLLISION & POLISH

Village-only changes:
- Added district-specific invisible walk-path collision masks.
- The Hunter can now move only on cobblestone roads, bridges, gate approaches, and central plazas.
- Movement uses axis-separated collision for smooth sliding along road edges.
- Water, grass parcels, walls, rooftops, and building areas are blocked.
- Citizens now use the same navigation mask as the Hunter.
- Citizen patrol routes were revised to axis-aligned road-safe waypoints so they no longer cut diagonally across grass or structures.
- Existing district gate teleport travel, camera controls, town building system, and saves remain intact.
- No battle, campaign, cards, hunters, relics, economy, or other game mechanics were changed.
