THE VILLAGE V28.2

- Restored the original combat-radius calculation for every non-Axe tower.
- Removed the Range upgrade option from all non-Axe towers.
- Axe Tower alone can purchase +0.50 tile attack-radius upgrades.
- Shared Battle XP now opens a visible upgrade-selection window whenever a Battle Level is earned.
- The upgrade window pauses combat, lists every placed attack tower, and lets the player choose Power, Speed, or Axe Range.
- Upgrade Points may be saved for later. Tap the UPGRADES HUD pill to reopen the window.
- Existing saves and permanent progression remain unchanged.
