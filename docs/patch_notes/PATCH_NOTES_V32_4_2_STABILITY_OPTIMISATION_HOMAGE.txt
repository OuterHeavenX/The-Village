THE VILLAGE — V32.4.2
STABILITY, OPTIMISATION & HOMAGE AUDIT

FIXED
- Removed 126 lines of unreachable legacy Village controller from the battle
  module. villageBootstrap.js has been the sole Village owner since V30.1; the
  old copy's guard clause meant none of it could ever run. This also removed a
  duplicate clampCamera definition.
- Fixed a stale stylesheet cache identifier. index.html still asked for
  styles.css?v=v27-8-shadow-trail-fix, so Safari could pair a V27.8 stylesheet
  with V32.4 markup after an update. All cache identifiers now move together.
- Removed applyBooster(), which no card could reach, along with a latent bug
  inside it that capped gate healing at 30 HP and could reduce health on keeps
  upgraded past that.
- Removed the write-only G.gold field.
- Gate health no longer shows fractions in the HUD after heal traps or the
  Faerie familiar (it read 18.35 instead of 19).
- End-of-run chests no longer throw when the eligible card pool is empty.
- Removed an empty assets/backgrounds folder and corrected package-lock.json,
  which was still recording version 27.3.0.

FASTER
- The road path set and enemy route points are now memoised behind a road
  version counter. The path set was rebuilt on every pointer-move during
  placement, and route points were rebuilt for every enemy on every frame in
  both the simulation and the renderer.
- The tactical grid draws as one batched path instead of 288 separate rectangle
  strokes per frame.
- Sprite preloading is split by when you can actually meet a sheet. About
  0.87 MB of chapter 11+ enemy art no longer competes with first paint; it is
  warmed once the browser is idle.
- Removed a dead sprite lookup table that only duplicated existing preloads.

NEW — THREE METAL GEAR SOLID HOMAGES
- CODEC, FREQUENCY 140.85. Type 14085, or triple-tap the weather readout during
  a hunt. Four callers, rewritten as advice about roads, tower adjacency and
  Essence. Your first ever call is answered with "Kept you waiting, huh?".
- ALERT. When a road guardian first notices you, the "!" rises above it.
- PSYCHO MANTIS. Five quick taps on the Profile stat grid. He reads your actual
  save file back to you — your runs, your victories, the card you hoard most,
  your best wave — then asks you to put your device down, and moves the screen.

KNOWN — NOT CHANGED
- Soul Reserve, Hunter Tavern and the village Night Market/Tavern starting-soul
  bonuses currently do nothing; they fed only the removed gold field. Repairing
  them would meaningfully change early-wave balance, so that is left as a design
  decision. See project_docs/AUDIT_V32_4_2.md.
