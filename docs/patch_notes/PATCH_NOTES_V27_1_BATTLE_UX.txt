THE VILLAGE — V27.1 BATTLE UX

Focused, low-risk UX update built from the confirmed V26.4 gold build.

- Camera can pan with one finger while a tower/card is being placed.
- Pinch zoom remains available during placement.
- Dragging no longer accidentally places a tower; tapping still confirms placement.
- Placement bar now explains drag-to-pan and pinch-to-zoom.
- Tower selection has a larger touch target and a stronger pulsing range/selection indicator.
- Three-card Essence draft keeps every card icon and now shows readable Level, Damage, Attack Rate, Range/Effect, Rarity, and placement cost.
- Draft typography, spacing, contrast, and responsive phone/iPad layouts improved.
- Combat balance, saves, waves, controls, and tower auto-leveling were not changed.
