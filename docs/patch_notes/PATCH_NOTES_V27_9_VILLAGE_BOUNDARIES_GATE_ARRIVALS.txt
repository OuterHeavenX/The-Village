THE VILLAGE — V27.9 VILLAGE BOUNDARIES & GATE ARRIVALS

Village-only fixes:
- Restored hard movement boundaries around every district map.
- Shadow can no longer walk into transparent space, cross perimeter walls, or enter neighboring maps without a gate.
- Movement uses axis-separated collision for smooth wall sliding.
- Entering Old Town's east gate now places Shadow on the west side of the East District.
- Returning from the East District places Shadow at Old Town's east side.
- Entering Old Town's west gate now places Shadow on the east side of the West District.
- Returning from the West District places Shadow at Old Town's west side.
- North/south gate behavior remains unchanged.
- No battle, cards, economy, progression, enemy, or balance systems changed.
