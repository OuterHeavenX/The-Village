THE VILLAGE — V28.0 GOLEM BOSS ARC / SHADOW LEVEL 2

- Campaign levels 1–4 now use the Golem's first transformation.
- Campaign levels 5–9 now use the Golem's second transformation.
- Level 10 final-wave Golem begins in form 1 and transforms at 67% and 34% health.
- The third transformation destroys one currently placed tower every 6 seconds.
- Completing level 10 for the first time drops the unique Heart of the Golem once only.
- Heart of the Golem permanently raises Shadow to level 2 and activates the supplied level-2 sprite in battle and the Village.
- Existing saves are migrated additively; cards, towers, merges, maps, upgrades, inventory, and progression are preserved.
- No unrelated combat balance or Village mechanics were changed.
