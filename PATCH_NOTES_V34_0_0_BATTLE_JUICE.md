# V34.0.0 — Battle Juice

Built from the stable V33.0.3.1 Battle Level Recovery master.

## Battle-only polish
- Critical hits use gold impact treatment and a brief hit pause, with no routine camera shake.
- Camera shake is reserved for boss entrances, boss attacks, transformations, tower-destruction events, and boss deaths.
- Added projectile motion trails and retained distinct dagger, axe, cross, scripture, familiar, arrow, and whip rendering.
- Added stronger tower recoil/fire flashes and tower-specific firing audio cues.
- Added type-aware enemy death particles, including spectral lift and bone/stone debris.
- Added visible tower upgrade tier accents, banners at high levels, and existing level stars.
- Added rain impact splashes across wet battlefields.
- Improved boss phase impact, boss death feedback, and cinematic hit pauses.
- Preserved existing boss introductions, transformations, weather, victory sequence, relic rewards, essence pickups, combos, and enemy animation states.

## Safety
- No Village code, navigation, Save Manager, campaign unlocks, card data, tower balance, resources, or save keys were changed.
- JavaScript syntax checked with Node.
